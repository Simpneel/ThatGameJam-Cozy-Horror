# Game Design (build target: itch.io)

2D point-and-click cozy-horror spot-the-difference. The player is stuck in a loop of days at home.
The house starts partly empty; buying items on the computer fills it, and the things in it start to go wrong.

## Day loop

1. **Day start**: the transition video plays, then the day's music starts.
   - Days 1–4 play `MUS_Day_01..04`. From Day 5 on, a random track plays each day.
2. **Explore**: the camera shows one room at a time. Arrow buttons pan left and right.
   - Clicking a normal object makes it wiggle and play its sound. Misclicks are harmless.
   - Clicking an **anomaly** snaps it back to normal and gives +1 hidden point.
   - Clicking the **computer** opens the shop.
   - Clicking the **bed** asks "Go to sleep?". Yes ends the day.
3. **Bed**: the game checks for a win or loss, then the next day starts.

## Anomalies

- Day 1 has none. From Day 2, new anomalies appear each day, and the number rises per day.
- Unresolved anomalies **persist** into the next day, so the house gets creepier if you ignore it.
- Anomalies can happen to base furniture, not just bought items, so there is something to fix before any purchases.

## Points and shop (hidden)

- Points are never shown to the player.
- The player starts with enough points for one purchase (the Day 1 shopping tutorial).
- The shop allows up to 4 purchases per day, limited by points.
- Unaffordable items look normal. Clicking one shows plain white **"CAN'T AFFORD"** text on a dark grey background.
- A bought item appears in the room with a pop-in.

## Flyer

- It rolls once per day. Chance = 15% on Day 1, +10% per day.
- Days 1–3: clicking it just dismisses it.
- From Day 4, clicking the flyer opens the shop.

## Endings (both hidden)

- **Win**: after the player has earned 5 points in total, 3 days in a row with no purchase wins at bedtime.
- **Lose**: owning every item ends the game in a loss at the next bedtime.

## Current tuning (all editable in the Inspector on the `Game` object)

| Setting | Value | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Starting points | 2 | GameManager |
| Points needed to arm the win streak | 5 | GameManager |
| Points per anomaly fixed | 1 | Prop |
| Max purchases per day | 4 | Shop |
| New anomalies per day | 2 on Day 2, +1 each day, max 5/day, max 9 at once | AnomalyDirector |
| Mid-day surprise anomaly (in a room you're not looking at) | from Day 3, 25–55 s in | AnomalyDirector |
| Flyer | 15% +10%/day, once per day, 12–40 s in; opens the shop from Day 4 | FlyerPopup |

**Shop catalogue** (the card art prints prices 1/2/4/2, so a slot's price is fixed):
Page 1: Flower Vase 1, Radio 2, Headphones 4, Board Game 2.
Page 2: Plant 1, Lamp 2, Smart Speaker 4, Kettlebell 2.
Page 3: Goat Button 1. Total = 19 points.

Every purchase also drops a delivery box onto the hallway rack.

**Anomalies:** items with drawn anomaly art (vase, board game, plant, lamp, kettlebell, goat, bedroom rug, kitchen vent) usually swap to it.
Anything else gets a procedural change: tilt, upside-down, shift, grow, shrink or red tint. The bed and the monitor never become anomalies.

**Rooms** (← → arrows or A/D): Bedroom → TV Room → Kitchen → Hallway → Bathroom → Balcony → (wraps).

## How it's built

- **Rebuilding the scene:** `Assets/Scenes/Game.unity` is generated by **Cozy Horror ▸ Rebuild Game Scene** (`Assets/Editor/CozyBuilder.cs`).
  Item placement lives in `ShopSpecs` there. Re-running the builder replaces manual scene edits, so change placement there.
- **Builds:** **Cozy Horror ▸ Build ▸ WebGL (itch.io)** or **Windows**. Output goes to `Builds/` (git-ignored).
- **Debug keys** (Editor and Development builds only): 1 = +5 points, 2 = spawn an anomaly, 3 = show the flyer, 4 = show the hidden-state readout.

## Screens

Start screen → game → (pause: Resume / Credits / Quit) → Win screen or End screen → Credits.
There is no save file; every launch is a fresh run.
