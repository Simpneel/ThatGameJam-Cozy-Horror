WHILE STOCKS LAST! - AILMENTS / DEBUFFS (everything that makes life harder)
===========================================================================

CREEP: unfixed anomalies pile up (max 10 at once)
-----
New anomalies each morning from Day 2: 3, 5, 7, 9 (then 9/day, never past the cap of 10), plus 1-4
"mid-day surprises" from Day 3 in rooms you aren't looking at. Unfixed ones
carry over to the next day.

  Unfixed  Effect
  -------  ------------------------------------------------------------------
  1+       Dark vignette closes in and the music slowly detunes (full at 8).
  5+       LIGHTS FLICKER every 5-10 s, with a buzz.
  7+       THINGS VANISH: an object in the room you're looking at disappears
           every 20 s (max 2 gone at once). Click the empty spot to bring it
           back (counts as a fix, +1 point).
  9+       EXHAUSTION: stay at 9+ for 20 s -> warning caption, then you're sent
           to bed and the day ends. Fixing anomalies drops you under 9 and
           resets the timer. Forced sleep counts as a normal bedtime for the
           win/lose check.

Anomalies also give themselves away: every 7-14 s one twitches and makes its
sound (faint if it's in another room).

ESCALATION
----------
- Anomalies get stronger each day: mild on Day 2, full strength by Day 6
  (bigger tilts, bigger resizes). Red tint only appears from Day 4.
- Hand-drawn anomalies are favoured (12x before Day 5, 4x after).

ECONOMY
-------
- PRICE HIKE (Day 5-10): every price doubles (1/2/4/2 -> 2/4/8/4).
  "PRICE HIKE! EVERYTHING COSTS DOUBLE" when you open the shop.
- DOUBLE PRICE HIKE (Day 11+): every price triples (-> 3/6/12/6).
  "DOUBLE PRICE HIKE!! EVERYTHING COSTS TRIPLE".
  The card art keeps showing the old prices.
- STALE ANOMALIES: unfound for 3 days -> fixing pays 0 points (dull thunk).
- COOLDOWN: a fixed object can't go wrong again until the day after next,
  so you can't farm one object.

TEMPTATION (pushes you to buy, which works against winning)
----------
- Computer pings every 25-40 s from Day 2 while you have points and haven't
  opened the shop today.
- Flyers: guaranteed once on Days 1-3; from Day 4, 2-3 chances a day and
  clicking one opens the shop. Anomaly flyers (Day 2+) give +1 point instead.
- House voice: the TV, laptop, radio and smart speaker chatter ads every
  25-45 s. Pushier on Days 3-4, uneasy from Day 5.

TIME
----
- A day lasts 120 s: Morning -> Afternoon -> Evening -> Night, darker each step.
  The clock keeps running in the shop and menus (only pause and room changes
  stop it). Nothing forces bed at night; the bed just starts glowing and nudging.

All numbers are editable in the Inspector (Game object: AnomalyDirector,
Atmosphere, Shop, GameManager, Prop). Full table in DESIGN.md.
